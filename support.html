Support

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

QUICKLOG is a free iPad flight-log and briefing notepad. This page is the support address for the Home Screen web app and the native iPad app.

In the future, we will add a FAQ to this page, but most of the app is self-explanatory for pilots familiar with the EFB/OFP environment.

On the native iPad app, AirDrop sends the last flight, departure and arrival text, and the crew rest table to another iPad that already has QUICKLOG. The transfer is device-to-device; iPads without the app cannot open it, and nothing is uploaded to a server.

You can also copy Crew Rest details for each rest to your clipboard and paste it into a new note in the company OFP app. Then, it can be synchronized to the other pilots' OFP apps so everyone has the same Rest table.

Common notes

  • Log lines and form values stay on the device. Deleting the Home Screen web app or uninstalling the iPad app clears them.
  • Back up marked log lines with COPY MARKED AS TABLE or CSV before you clear the logbook.
  • The web app needs an online open once to cache. After that it is meant to work in airplane mode.
  • Crew-rest wake-up alerts on the web app only fire while QUICKLOG stays open. The native iPad app can use a system notification at rest end.
